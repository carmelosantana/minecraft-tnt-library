TNT LIBRARY
Art direction for six custom explosives. Each is signature-colored but still reads instantly as Minecraft TNT — banded caps, a center label band, and a one-pixel dark outline on every face.

WATER BOMB
Explodes, then floods the crater to the rim. Blue caps, watery body.
THE TWINS
Inverse pair. Bands mark a line; detonate what's between them.
F-BOMB
Formidi-bomb lure. Cracked obsidian, cursed purple glow — summons a boss.
G-BOMB
Gravity kill. Void indigo with suspended motes — float, then slam.
SMART BOMB
Command-block look. Etched circuits, glowing core — programmable.
WHITE OUT
The scariest. Featureless white — vacuums everything in, flings it out.
STILL READS AS TNT
Darker banded caps on top & bottom · a horizontal label band across the side · a 1-px darker outline on every face. Signature color and motif change; the TNT silhouette does not.
PIPELINE
